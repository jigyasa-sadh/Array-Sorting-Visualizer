ARRAY SORTING VISUALIZER

How to Use This Visualizer
  1. Choose Your Array: Enter a Size (2–20) and type your numbers separated by commas or spaces, then click Set Array — or simply click Random Array to generate numbers automatically.
  2. Pick Algorithm & Order: Select a sorting algorithm, choose Ascending or Descending, and pick a speed (Slow / Medium / Fast).
  3. Watch & Learn: Click Sort Array to see step-by-step comparisons, swaps/shifts, and live explanations in the status box.
